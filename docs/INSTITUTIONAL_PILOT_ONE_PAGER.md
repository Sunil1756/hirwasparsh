# GREEN ENLIGHTENMENT
## Digital Ecological MRV & Verifiable Plantation Intelligence

> **Transforming Reforestation & CSR Afforestation into Immutable, Evidence-Backed Natural Capital.**

---

### Executive Summary
Traditional tree plantation drives suffer from an estimated **40% to 70% mortality rate** within the first 12 months, largely unmonitored due to paper-based tracking, manual sampling errors, and the absence of digital proof. 

**Green Enlightenment** provides an end-to-end digital **Measurement, Reporting & Verification (MRV)** platform. Every planted tree receives an immutable digital twin, unique serialized identifier (`GE-YYYY-NNNNNN`), multi-modal AI species verification, and ongoing multi-spectral monitoring via the European Space Agency's **Copernicus Sentinel-2** constellation.

### 3-Tier Digital MRV Architecture

```
[ Tier 1: Ground Truth ] ──> [ Tier 2: AI & Anti-Fraud ] ──> [ Tier 3: Satellite Telemetry ]
  • Sub-5m GPS Precision       • Gemini 2.5 Botanical Vision   • Copernicus Sentinel-2 Level-2A
  • 3-Angle Photo Geotag       • Perceptual dHash Checks       • 10m BOA NDVI & SAVI Trends
  • Planter Biometric Selfie   • Species Suitability Matrix    • Verra VM0047 Carbon Biomass
```

1. **Tier 1 — Ground Truth Evidence**: High-resolution camera acquisition capturing pit preparation, planted sapling, and planter identity with hardware EXIF timestamps and sub-5m GPS coordinates.
2. **Tier 2 — Multi-Modal Vision AI**: Google Gemini botanical vision screens for genuine in-ground planting, calculates environmental authenticity scores, and verifies species suitability.
3. **Tier 3 — Copernicus Sentinel-2 Remote Sensing**: Ongoing satellite overpasses compute 10m Bottom-Of-Atmosphere (BOA) vegetation indices (NDVI, SAVI, NDRE, EVI) to verify canopy expansion over 36 months.

#### For CSR Corporate Sponsors & ESG Officers:
- **Zero-Greenwashing Compliance**: Real-time dashboard (`/csr-portal`) tracking live survival rates, tree health breakdown, and audited metric tons of $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$ sequestered.
- **Audit-Ready ESG Disclosures**: Downloadable BRSR / GRI-compliant project reports with tamper-proof certificate URLs (`/verify/cert/:serialNo`).
- **Brand Storytelling**: Interactive GIS map embeds displaying exact geo-locations of corporate-sponsored trees.

#### For NGOs, Universities & Forestry Departments:
- **Dedicated NGO Command Center (`/ngo-workspace`)**: Manage teams, assign field scouting tasks, and track ground worker KPIs.
- **Offline Field Mode**: Scouts can register trees in remote, zero-connectivity forest areas with automatic background synchronization upon returning to network coverage.
- **DPR & Boundary Tools**: Export standard KML, KMZ, and GeoJSON boundaries for state forest compliance.
